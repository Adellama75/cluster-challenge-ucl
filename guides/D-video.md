# Role D: video lead

You own the 5-minute video: the script, the recording plan and the edit. That's video quality (5),
cloud and security (10) and team work (15), and you hold the whole 35 + 15 video marks together.
Video: Q1, Q2, Q8, plus intro and outro.

Run `/script` after each session, with a line of notes.

## Day 1: script skeleton and research

1. **Open `video/script.md`.** It has one section per question with a time budget (about 700 words at
   140 words per minute). Teammates' `/script` runs fill it in as they work; you shape it into one script.
2. **Research Q1 and Q2** and write your draft lines:
   - *Q1, cloud compute:* computing resources (servers, storage) rented on demand over the network
     instead of owned; you get a virtual machine like ours on Condenser in minutes and pay for or share
     what you use. Mention IaaS (our VM) vs PaaS/SaaS in a sentence at most.
   - *Q2, security:* who can access the machine (strong passwords or keys, no shared logins), data at
     rest and in transit (encryption, SSH), least privilege (file permissions, which A handles in Q3),
     where the data physically lives (data residency, GDPR), patching, and that the provider and the
     user share responsibility.
3. **Choose the recording tool** with the team (Panopto, Teams or Clipchamp; the brief suggests these).
   Do a 20-second test recording with screen and voice and check the audio.

## Days 2 to 3: storyboard

4. **Storyboard:** for each segment, who speaks, what is on screen, and how long. Screens must be
   readable: zoom terminals to a large font and crop figures.
5. **Teamwork (Q8) is the biggest single block (15 marks).** Read `video/teamwork-log.md` and pick two or
   three real problems and how we solved them. Concrete beats generic ("the first package install failed
   on the VM because…, A fixed it by…").
6. **Lock lines** as they're agreed: add `<!-- locked -->` at the end so `/script` won't change them.

## Thursday: record

7. **Record in short clips, one per question**, each by its owner (or voice-over on screen recordings
   for anyone who'd rather not be on camera). Re-recording a 30-second clip is easy; re-cutting a long
   take isn't.
8. Read from the script. Aim for each clip at or slightly under its budget.

## Friday by 18:00: edit and submit

9. **Edit:** clips in order, a title card with the team name and members, simple cuts. Total length
   **5:00** (check the brief's wording on tolerance; aim for 4:50–5:00).
10. **Export MP4**, play it back once start to finish (audio, readability), then **upload to the team's
    CIUK Challenge folder**.
11. Tell the group it's uploaded, with the link.
